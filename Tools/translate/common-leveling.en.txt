The Blackthorne camps
The Blackthorne cultists camp on the slopes north-east of the Summit (level 60, with elite Outrunners, Priests and a Courier among them: pull carefully). {npc:260674}, an elite (level 60) who leads them, stands among them: leave him be. Ghansurok, an elite chimaera (level 61), also roams the ridge north of the camps.
