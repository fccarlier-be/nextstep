NextStep : données importées de Polestar
========================================

Les fichiers de données de ce dossier sont générés par Tools/import/import.lua de
NextStep (https://github.com/fccarlier-be/nextstep) à partir de Polestar 0.3.0
(https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar), puis convertis au format de NextStep.

# Credits of Ding's game data

The files of this folder (`Ding/DB`) are generated by `tools/build_data.lua` from the database
of **Questie** and **QuestieDB**:

- Questie: https://github.com/Questie/Questie (published under the GNU General Public License
  v3 on https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questie);
- QuestieDB: https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB, whose data and schema come from Questie.

Thanks to their authors and contributors. Like Ding, these files are released under the GNU
General Public License v3 (see `LICENSE` at the root of Ding's repository): you may use,
change and share them under the same terms.

What changed from the source: only the fields Ding reads are kept, the spawns are converted from
AreaIDs to UiMapIDs (two decimals), and the names in each language are kept in one file per
language. The release used is written at the top of each file (`Ding.DB.source`). DingDB's own
corrections and additions (`tools/data/corrections` in Ding's repository) are applied on top;
the weapon skills of the weapon masters were checked against the trainer lists of
[cMaNGOS classic-db](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db) (GNU General Public License v3).

Names and texts of the game belong to Blizzard Entertainment.
